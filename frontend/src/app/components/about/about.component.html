Our Story

Built for Startups & Growing Businesses

Infort is a full-stack technology and growth partner for startups and growing businesses. We bring engineering, design, and marketing together under one team, so you don't have to coordinate between five different vendors to get things done.

On the technical side, we build and maintain your websites, custom software, and ERP systems — designed to grow with you, not just launch and sit still. On the digital side, we build your audience and your pipeline through SEO, content, social media, and performance marketing. From your first line of code to your next campaign, one team handles it end-to-end.
